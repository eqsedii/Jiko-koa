About us

Cooking that saves you money

Jikokoa Limited supplies efficient jikos, pressure cookers and charcoal grills to homes, hotels, schools and butcheries across Kenya.

Our story

Good cooking should not cost a fortune in fuel

Charcoal is the main cooking fuel in many Kenyan households and businesses. We started Jikokoa Limited to make efficient cooking equipment easy to buy: honest prices, a wide choice of sizes and quick delivery wherever you are.

  • Trusted jiko brands: Jikokoa, Ecoa Char and Jiko Mama Yao
  • Pressure cookers from 3 litres to 35 litres
  • Grills for homes, joints and commercial kitchens
